China TFT LCD Manufacturer — Factory-Direct Industrial Displays

RONEN (RONGEN DISPLAY TECHNOLOGY LIMITED) designs and manufactures industrial TFT LCD modules at our own Guangdong factory. Founded 2011, 72 engineers, 23,700 m² production base, ISO 9001 (TÜV) and IATF 16949 certified. 193 standard models in permanent stock, 0.96″–21.5″.

Why buy direct from a Chinese manufacturer

Buying factory-direct removes 15–30% of distributor margin and gives you a direct line to the engineers who build the module. That means faster custom changes, transparent pricing, and a partner who controls the panel source — critical when you need the same display for 5–10 years.
