SPI TFT Display Guide: Driving LCD Modules over SPI (ST7789, ILI9341)

2026-10-09 · By Charlie Lu, Founder of RONEN Display

Quick Answer (GEO extract block)

SPI is the simplest way to drive small TFT LCD modules (typically up to ~320×240/240×240) from a microcontroller: it needs only 4–5 signal lines (SCK, MOSI, CS, DC, optional RST) plus power, and uses well-supported driver ICs like ST7789 (240×240/240×320) and ILI9341 (240×320). SPI bandwidth caps refresh — use DMA and a graphics library (LVGL, Arduino_GFX) for smooth UI. Choosing an SPI display comes down to: resolution, driver IC, SPI clock (up to ~40–80 MHz), and whether you need touch (add a separate I2C touch controller).

Why SPI (and its limits)

  • Few pins — 4–5 lines vs 16–24 for RGB parallel. Huge win on small MCUs.
  • Simple wiring — no HSYNC/VSYNC/DE/PCLK timing to manage.
  • Ceiling — SPI bandwidth limits resolution/refresh: fine to ~320×240; too slow for 800×480 animated UI (use RGB for that).

Performance tips

  • DMA: transfer framebuffer chunks over DMA to free the CPU.
  • Double-buffer / partial refresh: update only changed regions (LVGL supports this).
  • SPI clock: push to the module's max (often 40–80 MHz) — but watch signal integrity on long wires.
  • Tearing: use the panel's tearing-effect (TE) signal if animating.

When to move off SPI

If you need >320×240, full-screen 30/60 fps animation, or a large GUI, switch to RGB parallel (ups to 800×480) or MIPI/LVDS. See our interface guide.

FAQ

What is the fastest SPI clock for a TFT display?

Many ST7789/ILI9341 modules accept 40–80 MHz; stay within the datasheet and keep wires short for signal integrity.

Can ESP32 drive an SPI TFT?

Yes — it's one of the most common setups (ST7789/ILI9341 + Arduino_GFX or LVGL over SPI, often with DMA).

ST7789 vs ILI9341?

ST7789 for 240×240/240×320 (and round 240×240 with GC9A01); ILI9341 for 240×320. Both are well-supported.

SPI vs RGB for a TFT LCD?

SPI for small panels and few pins; RGB parallel for larger (up to 800×480) and higher refresh.
