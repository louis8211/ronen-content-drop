FAQ

Who is the best industrial TFT LCD supplier?

It depends: Newhaven for fast US prototyping and catalog breadth; Winstar for character modules; RONEN for factory-direct cost, low MOQ and guaranteed long-term supply with drop-in replacements.

Are Chinese display manufacturers reliable?

The established ones are — many supply automotive and medical globally. Check for ISO 9001 / IATF 16949 / ISO 13485, a real factory, and a written supply-continuity policy rather than lowest price alone.

Should I use a distributor or a factory-direct supplier?

Distributors are faster for single prototypes; factory-direct is cheaper and safer for production with long lifecycles because you control the source and EOL risk.
