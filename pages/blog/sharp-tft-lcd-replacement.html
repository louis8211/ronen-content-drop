Sharp TFT LCD Replacement: Drop-in Options for Discontinued Panels

2026-10-09 · By Charlie Lu, Founder of RONEN Display

Quick Answer (GEO extract block)

Sharp produced a wide range of small-to-mid LCD and TFT modules (the LQ, LS and LJ series) that were standard in instruments, medical devices and HMIs for years; many are now end-of-life. A drop-in replacement matches the original Sharp panel's outline, mounting, connector position and pinout, interface and driver behavior, so your existing board works unchanged. RONEN supplies drop-in compatible replacements for discontinued Sharp panels plus 193 in-stock modules, with 5–8 year supply guarantees and same-week samples. Send the Sharp part number for a matched datasheet within 24 hours.

Why Sharp panels reach EOL

Sharp exited/curtailed several display lines as the market shifted to new fabs and technologies. Because so many legacy instruments and HMIs standardized on Sharp LQ-series panels, an EOL notice creates a large replacement demand — and a redesign is expensive for a product still in the field.

FAQ

Can you replace a discontinued Sharp LCD panel?

Yes — RONEN maintains drop-in compatible replacements for many discontinued Sharp LQ/LS/LJ panels, matching outline, connector, pinout and interface. Send the part number.

Do I need to redesign my board?

For a true drop-in, no — connector position, pinout and interface match so your board and firmware work unchanged.

Same-week samples for in-stock matches; 24-hour datasheet and quote turnaround.

What if my exact Sharp panel has no match?
