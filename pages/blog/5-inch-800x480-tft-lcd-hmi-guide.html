5.0 inch 800x480 TFT LCD for HMI: Specs, Interface & Selection Guide

2026-10-09 · By Charlie Lu, Founder of RONEN Display

Quick Answer (GEO extract block)

The 5.0 inch 800×480 TFT LCD is the most widely used industrial HMI display size — it fits standard 5-inch panel cutouts, is readable at arm's length, and is natively supported by most display controllers (STM32 LTDC, i.MX, ESP32-S3). When specifying one, lock down five things: IPS panel (for off-axis viewing), brightness (250 nits indoor, 500+ for bright areas), RGB 24-bit parallel interface (the norm at this resolution), touch type (PCAP for bare finger, resistive/glove-mode for gloves), and operating temperature. RONEN stocks 5.0-inch 800×480 modules with these configurations and 5–8 year supply guarantees.

Why 5.0″ 800×480 became the industrial default

  • Fits standard cutouts — 5-inch mounting holes are an industry convention; swap-in is easy.
  • 800×480 is the sweet spot — enough pixels for a real GUI (buttons, fields, a small graph) without needing a heavy GPU.
  • Native RGB interface — STM32F7/H7 (LTDC), NXP i.MX RT, ESP32-S3 all drive 800×480 RGB directly, no bridge chip.

Factory HMIs with gloved operators → resistive or glove-mode PCAP. Clean control panels, bare finger → standard PCAP.

Wiring & backlight

  • Interface: 40-pin RGB FPC is typical (24-bit color + HSYNC/VSYNC/DE/PCLK + touch lines).
  • Backlight power: usually a separate 2-pin LED string (12V/24V via a boost driver) or integrated; PWM dimming on a dedicated pin.
  • Common gotcha: matching the FPC pinout to your controller board — confirm the connector pitch and pin order before committing.

FAQ

Is 800×480 enough for an HMI?

Yes for most — it fits ~40 readable characters per line and a multi-pane layout. For dense SCADA-style data, step up to 10.1″ 1024×600.

What interface does a 5-inch 800×480 use?

Almost always RGB 24-bit parallel (40-pin FPC). STM32 LTDC and i.MX RT drive it natively.

IPS or TN for a 5-inch HMI?

IPS — operators view HMIs from varying angles and TN shifts color/contrast off-axis.

Can I get a 5-inch 800×480 with capacitive touch?

Yes — PCAP is common at this size; choose glove-mode PCAP or resistive if operators wear gloves.
