FAQ

Why 1000 nits for sunlight?

At 100,000 lux, the 2:1 rule implies 50,000 nits — impossible for LCD. Optical bonding and anti-reflective coating cut reflected glare ~90%, making 1000 nits practically readable outdoors.

Does high brightness reduce lifespan?

Yes. 1000+ nit backlights run hotter and typically last 20,000–30,000 hours vs 50,000 for standard. Thermal design is critical.
