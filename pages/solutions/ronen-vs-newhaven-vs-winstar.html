RONEN vs Newhaven vs Winstar — Which Industrial TFT LCD Supplier?

Three of the most-searched industrial display suppliers, compared on the factors that actually decide your project: MOQ, price, lead time, end-of-life protection and certifications. This is a supplier-selection guide for OEM buyers and hardware engineers.

Quick verdict

All three are Taiwan/China-based industrial display specialists. Choose Newhaven for a huge catalog with US distribution, Winstar for character/ alphanumeric modules, and RONEN when you need factory-direct pricing, zero-to-low MOQ, guaranteed 5–8 year supply and drop-in replacements for discontinued Mitsubishi/Innolux panels.

Where each supplier wins

Newhaven has a very large catalog and strong US warehouse presence, which helps fast prototyping for North American engineers. Winstar is excellent for character and alphanumeric LCDs. RONEN wins on factory-direct pricing, low MOQ, and — most importantly — documented long-term supply and drop-in replacement modules, which is the number-one risk for products with 7–15 year lifecycles.

FAQ

Is RONEN cheaper than Newhaven?

Because RONEN is factory-direct with no distributor margin, unit pricing is typically 15–30% lower at comparable specs, especially above 500 pcs. Samples are low-MOQ.

Can RONEN replace a discontinued Newhaven or Mitsubishi panel?

Yes — RONEN maintains a drop-in compatibility library including replacements for Mitsubishi and Innolux standard displays. Send us the old part number.

What is RONEN's MOQ?

Effectively zero for standard in-stock models (1 pc sample, 10 pc batch). Custom ODM has project-based minimums.
